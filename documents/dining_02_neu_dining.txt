As a fourth-year who's been on and off the meal plan, here's the honest Northeastern dining rundown.

**International Village (IV)**
The crown jewel of NEU dining. International Village has rotating themed stations — the day I'm writing this it was Thai green curry, a Mediterranean mezze bar, and traditional American comfort food alongside. The quality is genuinely above-average for a university dining hall. The space itself is gorgeous: tall ceilings, warm lighting, feels less like a cafeteria and more like a food hall.

The all-you-can-eat format during swipe hours means you can eat a real meal without watching your dining dollars. Highly recommend for dinner. Weekend brunch here is also excellent — they do made-to-order waffles and a serious egg station.

**Stetson West**
More of a quick-service style dining hall. Great for grabbing a fast lunch between classes. The sandwich and wrap station is the best quick option on campus. The salad bar is well-stocked. Stetson East (across the courtyard) has a similar setup but slightly different daily menu — worth checking both on the app before you commit.

**The Ell Center Food Court / Outtakes**
Primarily uses dining dollars rather than swipes. Good variety of grab-and-go options. Outtakes has pre-made sushi, sandwiches, salads, and a hot food section. Price-per-item is higher if you're counting dining dollars, but the quality is better than the main halls for some items (particularly the sushi and grain bowls).

**Meal Plan Advice**
- First-year students on the residential plan get unlimited swipes at IV and Stetson, which is a great deal — use it aggressively.
- Dining dollars don't expire within the school year but do not roll over to the following fall.
- The Northeastern Dining app (DineOnCampus) shows the day's menus with full nutritional info and allergen labels.
- If you're going off co-op (and therefore off campus) for a semester, you can temporarily reduce or pause your meal plan — talk to the dining office in September before the deadline.

**Off-campus supplement**: Huntington Ave has great cheap eats near campus. El Pelon Taqueria on Peterborough St is a student favorite, and there's a Whole Foods on Westland for groceries. Many students buy a few staples to keep in their room and use the dining plan for main meals.
