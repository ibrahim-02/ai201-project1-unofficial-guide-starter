Title: BU dorm tier list — a completely honest ranking from someone who's lived in three of them
Subreddit: r/BostonU
URL: https://www.reddit.com/r/BostonU/comments/bu_dorm_tierlist
Score: 2105

Lived in Warren freshman year, moved to West Campus sophomore year, and did a semester in Stuvi2. Here's the real talk.

**S-Tier: StuVi2 (Student Village 2)**
If you can get into StuVi2, do it. Suite-style with 4–6 person suites, a shared common room with a TV, and a full kitchen. The rooms are newer and the building has in-unit laundry. It's on the East side of campus near Fenway which means: Fenway Park is 5 minutes away, the Kenmore T stop is right there, and there are tons of restaurants within walking distance. The tradeoff is it's farther from the main College of Arts & Sciences buildings on the West side, but the Green Line makes it manageable.

**A-Tier: The Towers (West Campus)**
525, 535, and 545 Comm Ave. Great location — right in the heart of the BU Strip (Commonwealth Ave), close to the B Line, and surrounded by restaurants. The rooms are traditional doubles with shared hall bathrooms. Not luxurious but the location makes up for it. The dining at Towers (Kilachand) is also convenient.

**B-Tier: Myles Standish Hall**
Central campus, quiet, good for people who want to focus. It's farther from the B Line than West Campus but walkable to almost everything on the main campus strip. The rooms tend to be slightly larger than West Campus doubles.

**C-Tier: Warren Towers**
The classic freshman experience. Enormous building, 1800+ students, it's basically a small city. The energy is intense — great if you want to meet people, bad if you ever want quiet. The rooms are very small (some of the smallest on campus). The dining hall in the building is convenient though. Warren puts you in the social deep end, which some people love and some hate.

**Off-Campus after Freshman Year**
Most upperclassmen move off campus. Allston and Brighton are the BU neighborhoods — expect $1,400–$2,000/month per room depending on how close to campus. Brighton is slightly cheaper. Look for apartments on Craigslist, Zillow, and the BU off-campus housing board (housing.bu.edu). Start looking in December–January for September moves, the good places go fast.

Key things to negotiate: who pays utilities (gas can be high in Boston winters), parking (rare and expensive), and laundry. Most apartments near BU don't include parking.
