I ate at every BU dining location at least five times this year. Here's the real breakdown.

**1. Marciano Commons (West Campus)**
Best overall dining hall at BU. The variety is excellent — there's always a pasta station, a stir-fry line, a grill, a soup bar, and a rotating international cuisine counter. The salad bar has 25+ toppings and is restocked frequently. Dessert is solid: usually two cakes, a cobbler, and soft serve. The seating area upstairs gets good natural light and is quieter than the main floor.

Downside: extremely crowded between 6–8 pm on weekdays. I've waited 20 minutes just to swipe in. Go for lunch on weekdays if you can — the same food, much shorter wait.

**2. Warren Towers Dining**
Convenient if you live in Warren but the selection is noticeably narrower. Great for a quick breakfast — eggs, toast, yogurt, bagels all available before 9 am. The made-to-order omelette station on weekend mornings is genuinely good. For dinner it's more repetitive, but they have a solid halal station that rotates dishes.

**3. West Campus Dining (Towers)**
Smaller and often overlooked. Worth going to for the build-your-own burrito bar which has better portions than Marciano. The allergen-free section here is the best-labeled on campus — critical if you have dietary restrictions. Vegan and gluten-free options are clearly marked and rotated weekly.

**Meal Plan Tips**
- The dining dollars roll over semester to semester (check your account at studentlink.bu.edu)
- You can use dining swipes at several cafes on campus, not just the halls — the George Sherman Union food court accepts them
- The "late night" hours at Marciano (10 pm–midnight) serve a limited but legit menu — good for post-library runs
- The BU Dining app shows current menus and has allergen filters — download it before move-in day

**Bottom line**: Marciano for variety, Warren for convenience, Towers for a calmer vibe. Avoid peak dinner times at all of them.
