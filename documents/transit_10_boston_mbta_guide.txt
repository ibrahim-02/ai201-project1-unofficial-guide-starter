Student at BU here but I've spent enough time near NEU and around the whole city to write a comprehensive transit guide for new students.

**The CharlieCard**
Get a physical CharlieCard at any T station (there's a machine at every major station). Load money onto it or buy a weekly/monthly pass. CharlieCard fare is $2.40 per subway ride; the MBTA app now lets you load your card digitally. Do NOT use cash or buy single-ride tickets — it's more expensive and slower at the gates.

**Green Line (BU)**
BU sits directly on the Green Line. The key stops:
- Kenmore: Eastern BU, connects to Fenway Park, StuVi1/2, and the B/C/D lines.
- BU Central: Middle of BU's main campus strip.
- BU East: Closest to the main academic buildings (CAS, Photonics, etc.).
- BU West: West Campus end of campus, closest to Agganis Arena.

The B Branch runs to Boston College; the C Branch to Cleveland Circle; the D Branch to Riverside. For getting to downtown (Back Bay, Copley, Government Center), take any of these branches inbound toward Park St.

**Orange Line (Northeastern)**
Northeastern is best served by the Orange Line:
- Ruggles: Main stop, directly adjacent to campus. Gets you downtown in ~10 minutes.
- Back Bay: One stop from Ruggles, connects to Amtrak and commuter rail if you need to leave the city.
- Forest Hills: The opposite end of the Orange Line; useful for Jamaica Plain.

Green Line E Branch also has a "Northeastern" stop on Huntington Ave — useful for the Museum of Fine Arts area.

**Commuter Rail for Weekend Trips**
North Station and South Station are the two commuter rail hubs. From South Station (Red Line or Silver Line) you can reach Providence, RI in 45 minutes; Salem, MA (Halloween tourism) via the Newburyport/Rockport line; and Plymouth in about an hour.

**Biking**
Bluebikes (Boston's bikeshare) has docking stations near both BU and NEU campuses. Student annual membership is discounted. Biking between BU and NEU along the Emerald Necklace path (or along Comm Ave → Huntington Ave) takes about 20 minutes and is a pleasant ride in good weather. Buy a good lock if you bring your own bike — bike theft in Boston is very real.

**Tips**
- Check the MBTA app (or Google Maps) for real-time arrival. The T is not always on schedule.
- The Green Line is the most unreliable line — especially the B Branch, which runs in traffic on Comm Ave. Budget extra time.
- During game nights at Fenway (Red Sox), the B/C/D Green Line gets absolutely packed. Walk from Kenmore if you're nearby rather than waiting for a train you can't board.
- The Silver Line to Logan Airport is free from the airport side — use it to get back to campus.
