Junior here. This is the guide I wish someone had given me before move-in day.

**Before You Arrive**
- Download the BU app and the MBTA app before you land. You will use both constantly.
- Buy a CharlieCard (MBTA transit card) at any T station. Don't buy single-ride tickets — the card is cheaper and faster. Load it at the fare machines or through the MBTA app.
- BU Student Link (studentlink.bu.edu) is your portal for class registration, financial aid, and dining dollars. Learn it in the first week.
- Set up your BU Kerberos login before move-in. IT can be slow to fix issues during the first week rush.

**Academic Life**
- Attend every class for the first two weeks, no exceptions. Professors notice early attendance patterns, and the first weeks establish the course's grading logic.
- Syllabus week is real — professors distribute syllabi and you need to read them carefully. Grade weights, drop policies, and late work rules are all in there.
- BU has a Pass/Fail option for one elective per semester. Use it strategically to explore a subject outside your major without risking your GPA.
- Office hours are not just for struggling students. Introducing yourself to a professor during office hours in the first month pays off at the end of the semester when you need a letter of recommendation.

**Social Life**
- Student Activities Fair at the beginning of each semester is worth attending. Sign up for more clubs than you think you want — you can always drop, but you can't join mid-semester in many cases.
- GSU (George Sherman Union) is the central hub. Food court, student organizations, lounges. Learn where it is on day one.
- The BU Hub (learning outcomes program) requires you to complete certain academic and co-curricular experiences. Plan for this early — some Hub requirements fill up fast.

**Practical Boston Stuff**
- The B, C, and D lines of the Green Line run along Commonwealth Ave through campus. The B Line (to Boston College) stops right on Comm Ave in front of BU. Get comfortable with it.
- CVS on Comm Ave is open late. There's also a Trader Joe's in Coolidge Corner (C Line, ~10 min ride) that has way better prices than the campus convenience stores.
- Boston winters are real. Buy a good coat before November. Campus is wind-tunneled in winter along Comm Ave — the gaps between buildings create brutal gusts.
- The Boston Public Library (Copley Square, Green Line D) is free for students and has excellent quiet study rooms you can book in advance. Better than the BU library during finals week when everything is packed.
